Install on phone or PC

Computer or Android: open this page in Chrome or Edge and choose Install app from the browser menu.

iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Installation requires an HTTPS website address (or localhost on this computer). On local Wi-Fi, open the phone address in your browser; full app installation may not be available over HTTP.

A live secure connection to the UCCHR server is required for attendance, payroll, and biometric operations.
